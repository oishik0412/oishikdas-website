About

Many interests.
One direction.

My professional experience has centred on solving complex problems where business, technology, products and people meet.

I have worked across consulting, SaaS, technology and project delivery, learning how ideas move from strategy into execution and eventually into outcomes.

That experience shaped a simple belief. Good work is not only about having the right answer. It is about understanding the problem deeply enough to build something that people can actually use and value.

Today, I am interested in the spaces where technology, business, research and human behaviour intersect.
