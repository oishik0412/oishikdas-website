ABOUT Oishik Das

Business · Technology · Ideas

Building a career
across disciplines.

I am interested in the space where business, technology, products and people meet.

THE PROFESSIONAL

My professional journey has taken me through consulting, technology, SaaS, project management, product delivery and business transformation.

Each experience has given me a different lens through which to understand organisations and the people working inside them.

I have worked across complex environments where execution matters, stakeholders are diverse and the distance between an idea and a successful outcome can be surprisingly large.

That experience has shaped how I think about products, adoption, strategy and execution.

THE THINKER

I have become increasingly interested in the questions behind the work.

Why do some products become indispensable while others struggle to gain adoption?

Why do organisations struggle to convert strategy into execution?

How can technology create meaningful improvements in the way people work and make decisions?

These questions have gradually led me towards research and the development of practical frameworks that connect theory with real organisational problems.

THE CREATIVE

There is another side of the journey that does not fit neatly into a professional résumé.

Acting, writing and music have remained important forms of expression and exploration.

Travel has added another dimension, creating an opportunity to observe different people, places, cultures and stories.

I see these interests not as separate identities, but as different ways of understanding the world.

Looking ahead

The goal is not
to fit into one box.

It is to build the range, depth and perspective required to create something meaningful.
