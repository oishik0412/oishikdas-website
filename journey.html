Journey

Every chapter
changed the lens.

From creative beginnings to engineering, consulting, management, technology and the ambition to build.

00

The journey has never been a straight line.

Each chapter added a different way of seeing the world. Creative expression taught me to observe people and stories. Engineering taught me to understand systems. Consulting taught me how organisations approach difficult problems. Management education expanded my understanding of strategy, finance, leadership and decision making. Technology brought me closer to products and the people who build, deliver and adopt them.

Today, my interests sit at the intersection of business, technology, product thinking and research, alongside a growing ambition to create things of my own and contribute at a larger institutional scale.

01

Creative beginnings

Kolkata

Where the creative side began.

Acting, music and writing became early forms of expression, curiosity and storytelling. Long before business and technology became professional interests, stories, performance and music offered a way to understand people and the world around me.

Acting Music Writing
02

Engineering

Bhubaneswar

Curiosity became structured thinking.

Engineering brought structure to curiosity. It taught me to think in systems, understand how things work and approach problems analytically. It became the foundation for the technology oriented way of thinking that would shape the chapters that followed.

Engineering Systems Analysis
03

Consulting

Hyderabad

Learning how organisations solve complexity.

Consulting became an education in business problem solving. Working with organisations and stakeholders taught me how strategy, execution and technology come together to solve complex business problems. It also showed me that the best solutions need to work in the real world, not only on paper.

Consulting Business Technology
04

Management education

Visakhapatnam

Understanding the business behind the system.

Management education added another layer to the journey, strengthening my understanding of strategy, finance, organisations, leadership and the decisions that shape businesses. It connected the technical and operational perspective with a broader understanding of how organisations create and capture value.

Strategy Finance Leadership
05

Technology and SaaS

Bengaluru

Where business and technology increasingly met.

Technology and SaaS brought me closer to products and the people who build, deliver and adopt them. Bengaluru became a place where business thinking, technology, product execution and customer outcomes increasingly came together.

SaaS Product Business Technology
06

The future

What comes next

The next chapter is about creating.

Building products. Developing ideas. Pursuing research. Exploring entrepreneurship. Continuing creative work. And looking for opportunities to create impact at a larger scale.
