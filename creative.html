THE CREATIVE SIDE

Not everything worth building has to be measured like a business.

Acting taught me to inhabit perspectives different from my own. Writing created a space to turn observations and ideas into words. Music became another form of expression.

These interests may appear different on the surface. The common thread is curiosity.
