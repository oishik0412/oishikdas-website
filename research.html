Research and inquiry

Questions worth

Research, for me, begins with a practical question: what can we understand better, measure more clearly or solve more effectively?

THE APPROACH

I am interested in research that connects rigorous thinking with real world problems.

My professional experience has exposed me to technology platforms, SaaS products, enterprise implementations, business transformation and product adoption.

These experiences continually generate questions about why organisations behave the way they do, why some products succeed while others struggle, and how better systems can improve outcomes.

Research provides a way to move beyond intuition, examine these questions systematically and develop ideas that can be challenged, tested and improved.
