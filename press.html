How IIM Visakhapatnam’s Executive MBA Is Shaping Enterprise Leaders Across Industries

A feature examining the evolution of executive education alongside the changing demands of enterprise leadership, technology and cross-functional business transformation.

Published by Poets&Quants for Execs, the feature places Oishik Das within IIM Visakhapatnam’s Executive MBA cohort and explores his professional journey across consulting, SaaS, CPaaS, CCaaS and fintech.

The feature connects his experience across HighRadius, Zluri, Kapture CX and Exotel with his perspective on leadership, technology and the role of executive education in preparing professionals for increasingly complex organisations.
