ABOUT Who I am

I work at the intersection of business, technology and ideas.

My professional journey has taken me across consulting, technology, SaaS, product and business transformation.

What connects these experiences is a simple interest: understanding how ideas become systems, how systems create outcomes, and how people ultimately experience them.

Alongside my professional work, I explore research and creative expression through writing, acting, music, travel and storytelling.

RESEARCH Questions · Frameworks · Evidence

Research & ideas

Curiosity,
made useful.

I am interested in turning practical business and technology problems into questions that can be examined, structured and understood.

My research explores themes around technology adoption, product behaviour, business transformation and the mechanisms through which organisations turn new ideas into sustained outcomes.

Some questions begin in the workplace. Others emerge from observation, experimentation or conversations. The goal is the same: develop ideas that are rigorous enough to study and practical enough to matter.
